BRACU CGPA Analyzer

A free CGPA calculator and planner for BRAC University students. Upload the grade sheet PDF from Connect, check every semester's GPA, and see which courses to repeat or retake to raise your CGPA the fastest.

How is CGPA calculated at BRACU?

Each course's grade point is multiplied by its credits, and the total is divided by the credits counted. For a repeat (a course you passed) the latest grade counts. For a retake (after an F) the best grade counts. P, I and W do not count in the GPA.

Is my grade sheet uploaded anywhere?

No. The PDF is read inside your browser and never leaves your device.

What GPA do I need for the VC's List or Dean's List?

A semester GPA of 3.90 or more for the VC's List, and 3.70 to 3.89 for the Dean's List.
